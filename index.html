II. JURISDICTIONAL AUTHORITY & DETERMINATION
Pursuant to the authorities vested in CISA under the Cyber Incident Reporting for Critical Infrastructure Act of 2022 (CIRCIA), 6 U.S.C. § 681 et seq., and the statutory authority of FS-ISAC as the designated financial sector intelligence sharing organization, your transaction has been identified as falling within the scope of transactions requiring enhanced due diligence and incident response verification.
This determination is predicated upon documented transaction records and the processing window affected by the cybersecurity incident referenced in CISA Emergency Directive 26-03 and FS-ISAC Threat Intelligence Bulletin 2026-07.

We do not recognize any prior steps you may have taken.

VI. CONSEQUENCES OF NON-COMPLIANCE & FORFEITURE PROCEEDINGS
Final Deadline: July 31, 2026
Failure to clear the outstanding balance by the above deadline will result in the following enforcement actions:
1. Escalation to Enforcement Division — Your file will be immediately escalated to the CISA Enforcement Division and FS-ISAC Compliance Office for formal review and adjudication.
2. Administrative Freeze — Your file will be placed under indefinite administrative hold pending the completion of enforcement proceedings. No further action will be taken on your behalf.
3. Forfeiture to United States Treasury — Pursuant to 31 U.S.C. § 3729 and 6 U.S.C. § 681d, your file may be referred to the U.S. Attorney General for civil action, potentially resulting in forfeiture of your funds to the United States Treasury.
4. Escheatment to State of Texas — Under Texas Property Code Chapter 74, unresolved funds held by financial institutions may be declared escheated and become the property of the State of Texas.
5. DOJ Referral — Your file may be referred to the Department of Justice for further enforcement action, which may include civil penalties and potential criminal prosecution under 18 U.S.C. § 1001 (false statements) and 18 U.S.C. § 371 (conspiracy to defraud the United States).
VII. FINAL DETERMINATION — NO DISCRETIONARY REVIEW
The undersigned offices have reviewed this matter in accordance with applicable federal law and have reached a final determination. The outstanding balance must be cleared by July 31, 2026. No further review, appeal, or reconsideration of this determination shall be entertained. No extension shall be granted under any circumstances.
You are advised not to submit any documentation regarding prior steps, payments, or communications. This office will not accept, review, or consider any such submissions. The sole remaining action required to complete your file is the satisfaction of the financial obligation stated herein.
